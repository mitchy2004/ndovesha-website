Consulting Content It Creates

Professional services social media marketing is about demonstrating judgement in small pieces.

Short insights

A single idea explained clearly, in the space of a post someone will finish.

Lessons from engagements

Anonymised observations from client work that show what you have actually seen.

Commentary

Your view on developments in your field, while they are still being discussed.

Firm and people

Who clients would work with, and the experience they bring.

What Consultancy Content Has to Balance

Professional services sell judgement, which means the content has to demonstrate it rather than claim it.

Show thinking, not availability

Posts that explain how you approach a problem build credibility. Posts announcing that you have capacity do not.

Client confidentiality comes first

Examples usually need permission or genuine anonymisation. Recognisable detail about a live engagement is a risk not worth taking.

LinkedIn is probably the whole channel

For most consultancies one platform done properly beats four done occasionally. Decide where your buyers actually are.
