Frequently Asked Questions

Can I edit blog posts and captions after they are generated?

Yes. You can refine any draft by chatting with your Worker. Change tone, shorten paragraphs, add CTAs, or turn a blog into social captions until the copy matches your brand.

Do I need professional writing experience?

No. Ndovesha is built for founders, marketers, and small teams who need publish-ready content without hiring a full-time copywriter.

Are blog articles structured for SEO?

Yes. Ndovesha generates articles with clear headings, readable structure, and keyword-aware copy you can publish on your site or blog.

You stay in control: review the draft, adjust sections in chat, and publish when it is ready.

Can the AI write in my brand's voice?

Yes. Simply provide your brand guidelines, tone, or examples of your writing, and the AI will generate content that matches your style.
