Frequently Asked Questions

Which social media platforms can the coworker post to?

Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn. Connect the accounts you use, then choose which platforms each post goes to. Captions are written to suit the platform they are published on.

Do I get to approve posts before they are published?

Yes. Posts land in your content calendar as drafts, and you are emailed when they need a quick review. You can open any draft, check the image and caption, then approve and schedule it or reject it.

Can it create images and videos, or only captions?

All three. Describe what you want to promote and the coworker writes the caption and designs the image or produces the video to go with it, following the brand instructions you provide.

Can I choose when it posts and how it sounds?

Yes. You set the days and times you want to post, add topic ideas, and give brand instructions such as your tone and style. The coworker uses these to fill your calendar with posts that sound like your business.
